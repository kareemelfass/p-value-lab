1. Percentiles and quantiles: the same position

Suppose we arrange systolic blood-pressure measurements from lowest to highest. A percentile is a value below which a stated percentage of observations fall. A quantile expresses the same position on a 0–1 scale.

25th percentile = 0.25 quantile
50th percentile = 0.50 quantile
75th percentile = 0.75 quantile

4. Why does normal data produce a straight pattern?

For a normal-shaped sample, low, middle and high observed quantiles change in approximately the same systematic way as theoretical normal quantiles.

low ↔ low middle ↔ middle high ↔ high

Because the two sets of quantiles move together approximately linearly, the Q–Q points follow an approximately straight pattern.

Important: a random sample from a truly normal population is not expected to make a perfectly straight line.

7. Sample-size experiment

Keep the population normal and change only the number of observations. Generate repeated samples to see sampling variation.

The population is normal in every case. What changes is the random sample. Small samples, especially their tails, can look surprisingly irregular.
